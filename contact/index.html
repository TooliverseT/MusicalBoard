Contact

We welcome feedback about bugs, accessibility, and ideas for new practice tools. Because MusicalBoard is operated as a small project, we cannot guarantee a reply to every message, but we read thoughtful reports.

How to reach us

Please use the public contact or repository channels linked from your deployment (for example, the site footer or project page on GitHub) if available. If you are reporting a privacy concern, mention "Privacy" in the subject line so we can route it correctly.

For privacy-specific questions, also see the Privacy Policy.
