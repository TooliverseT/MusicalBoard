How to Use Piano Keyboard

Use this piano keyboard to play various notes and practice music.

Click each key to play the corresponding note, and press multiple keys simultaneously to play chords.

When the sustain button is activated, notes continue to sound even after releasing the keys.

Use the octave adjustment buttons to display keys in different pitch ranges.
