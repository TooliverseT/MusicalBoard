How to Use Pitch Controls

This strip is the control center for everything that needs the mic on MusicalBoard. It captures audio from your browser, runs the same analysis pipeline used by the pitch and amplitude views, and keeps transport controls in one place.

Recording writes a take you can play back, scrub, or export. Monitoring routes live input to your speakers or headphones (watch for latency and feedback). Sensitivity raises or lowers how aggressively quiet input is detected; speaker gain adjusts monitor and playback loudness.

  • Record: arm the microphone and capture a new take (used by the pitch plot and amplitude views on other pages too).
  • Stop: end recording and freeze the captured buffers for playback and visualization.
  • Monitor: pass-through listen to the live mic input with separate level from the main mix.
  • Play / Pause: audition the last recording and sync the analyzers to the playhead.
  • Download: export the captured audio; pick WebM, MP3, WAV, OGG, or M4A before saving.
  • Sensitivity & speaker gain: tune detection threshold and output level without leaving the tool.
  • Progress bar: shows elapsed time, supports seeking during playback, and mirrors record/playback state across the suite.

Hover any icon to read the inline tooltip. If a page hides the analyzer UI, the hidden PitchAnalyzer instance on this route still powers audio—open the dashboard widgets when you want the graphs.
